Separate single-topic projects generated from the live ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API on 2026-03-28. Every included study in this portfolio met the same registry-native gate: randomized interventional design, completed status, posted results, at least two arms, and at least one recorded numeric CT.gov outcome. Direct pooled contrasts are emitted only when the posted CT.gov metric can be safely reduced to counts or means and the arm structure supports a defensible comparator.
